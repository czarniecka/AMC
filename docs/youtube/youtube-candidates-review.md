# YouTube candidates for manual review

Prepared on 2026-10-10 from the local discovery export. Twenty purposively selected candidates: ten with Polish titles and ten with English titles. Language is inferred from titles and must be checked. Priority 1 indicates the first review batch, not approval.

These videos and their new comments have not been watched or reviewed. Titles, dates and comment counts are discovery metadata; counts can include replies. This shortlist is exploratory, not a representative sample or evidence of causal effects. Dates cover 2023–2026 unevenly; it cannot support a before/after-2023 comparison.

## Review checklist

- Confirm the spoken language and whether the video is a full discussion or a short clip.
- Check that AI and work are central; record a timestamp showing skill changes, employer expectations, adaptation pressure, or personal experience.
- Distinguish personal experience, expert interpretation, prediction and promotional claims.
- Check a few public comments for relevant experiences rather than spam or requests for resources. Do not copy author details.
- Mark Approve, Reject, or Unsure with a brief reason, reviewer and review date. All entries start as Pending.
